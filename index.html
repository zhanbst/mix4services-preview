Whole-house water softeners

Hard water ends here.

We provide complete whole-house water softener services throughout Greater Houston — from selecting the right equipment to professional installation, system startup and final testing.

A water softener removes the hardness-causing minerals, primarily calcium and magnesium, from your water. This helps prevent scale buildup throughout the home, protecting water heaters, plumbing fixtures, pipes and water-using appliances while helping them operate efficiently and last longer.

Softened water is also gentler on skin and hair, helping reduce dryness, tightness and the residue hard water can leave after bathing. It also means fewer white spots and soap scum on faucets, shower glass, tile and dishes, easier cleaning, and better performance from soap, shampoo and laundry detergent.

Most standard installations are completed, started up and tested the same day.

Under-sink reverse osmosis

Fresh, filtered water on demand.

Want cleaner, better-tasting water without constantly buying bottled water or relying on a basic refrigerator filter? An under-sink reverse osmosis system gives your family fresh, filtered water whenever you need it — for drinking, cooking, coffee, tea and ice — while reducing the expense, the inconvenience and the plastic waste of bottled water.
