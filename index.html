    Before you call

    Questions we get every week

    Do you charge for estimates?
    On-site estimates are free for projects over $1,000. For smaller repairs there is an $89 service call, and it is credited toward the work if you hire us.
    How fast can you get here?
    Weekday calls placed before 2pm usually get a same-day visit inside our core service area. Emergencies — active leaks, no power, storm damage — are answered around the clock.
    Are the prices on this site final?
    No. They are typical ranges for the Houston market, published so you know roughly where you stand before picking up the phone. Your final price depends on access, the age and condition of what we find, the materials you choose and your exact location. You get a fixed written price after the on-site assessment.
    Do you pull permits?
    Yes, wherever the work requires one. Permit fees appear as their own line on your estimate rather than buried inside the labour rate.
    What areas do you cover?
    Katy and West Houston first — Cypress, Fulshear, Richmond, Brookshire, the Energy Corridor, Memorial and Sugar Land. Call us for anything just outside that.
    Is there a warranty?
    Every completed job carries a 1-year workmanship warranty. Manufacturer warranties on materials and appliances pass through to you.
